About

Who we are

A buddy for the whole school journey.

TutorBuddy brings classroom teaching and AI study help onto one platform. Students learn from verified tutors online, or meet them in person across Delhi. When a tutor is not in the session, ब्रह्मांड Ai stays available for doubts, explanations, and practice — in English and Indian languages, including voice.

1

Learn the syllabus

Courses run from kindergarten through senior secondary and competitive exams. NCERT chapters, subjects, and PDFs are organised class by class.

2

Practise with AI

Explain a topic at the right level, snap a question, take a short quiz, or duel a friend. The tools sit next to the chapter, not in a separate app.

3

Meet a real tutor

Find a tutor, check availability, book a slot, pay securely, and talk in messages or a live session. Offline centres cover neighbourhoods across Delhi.

Built for students

A dashboard for bookings, messages, NCERT reading, progress, and the AI toolkit. Sign in with email or Google.

Built for tutors

A profile, availability, bookings, session tools, and a direct line to students. Teaching stays organised without a pile of separate apps.

What you can do

On the platform

From the first doubt to the booked class.

A student can study alone with AI, then bring the same topic to a tutor. A tutor can run the session with a board, a recording, and a message thread that stays after the call.

As a student

  • Create an account and open a personal dashboard.
  • Browse courses from kindergarten to competitive exams.
  • Read NCERT by class, subject, and chapter.
  • Ask ब्रह्मांड Ai, or open Magic Explain, Micro Quiz, Snap & Solve, and Doubt Battle.
  • Search tutors, compare profiles, and book a time.
  • Join the session online, or book an offline meet in Delhi.
  • Pay for the booking and message the tutor.
  • Track progress and revisit chapter summaries and flashcards.

As a tutor

  • Register, complete a profile, and set weekly availability.
  • Receive bookings and manage upcoming sessions.
  • Teach online with messaging beside the session.
  • Draw on the virtual whiteboard and record the screen.
  • Point students to quizzes, flashcards, and chapter notes.
  • Keep a record of who they teach and when they teach.

Study together

Study groups bring peers into one room with chat, video, and a shared board.

Switch the room

Learn from home, or sit with a tutor at centres in Lajpat Nagar, Rohini, Dwarka, Saket, Pitampura, and Janakpuri.

Ask in your language

AI teaching supports English, Hindi, Punjabi, Gujarati, Marathi, and Bengali, with voice alongside text.

Why TutorBuddy

Human teaching, with AI that does not clock out.

Most platforms pick one side: a tutor marketplace, or an AI chatbot. TutorBuddy keeps both, and puts the school syllabus between them.

01

One place for the whole week

Read the chapter, quiz it, ask the AI, then book the tutor — without sending the student to four different products.

02

Online and in the neighbourhood

A live class from home, or a face-to-face session at a Delhi centre when a screen is not enough.

03

NCERT, not a random feed

Classes, subjects, and chapters follow the school path students already have, from early years to Class 12.

04

Language that feels local

Explanations and voice support in Indian languages, so a doubt does not have to be translated before it can be asked.

05

Practice with a point

Magic Explain, micro quizzes, flashcards, photo solve, and doubt battles turn a chapter into something a student can finish.

06

Fair for both sides of the desk

Students get a clear booking and payment path. Tutors get availability, sessions, messages, a board, and recordings.

Future integrations

Planned connections

The platform grows into the tools families already use.

These integrations are on the roadmap. They are not in the product yet. Each one removes a step that still happens outside TutorBuddy.

Payments

UPI and Indian payment rails

Add UPI, net banking, and popular Indian wallets beside card checkout, so a booking can be paid the way families already pay.

Reminders

WhatsApp and SMS

Session reminders, tutor replies, and quiz nudges on WhatsApp and SMS, not only inside the website.

Calendar

Google Calendar sync

Push confirmed bookings onto the student and tutor calendars, and block times that are already taken.

Family

Parent accounts

A parent view of attendance, upcoming classes, payments, and a weekly study snapshot, without sharing the student’s login.

Schools

School and coaching admin

An institute portal for batches, assigned tutors, and shared NCERT progress across a class.

Classroom

Embedded video rooms

A native class room so video, the whiteboard, and chat live in the session page rather than a separate meeting link.

Mobile

iOS and Android apps

The same learning desk on a phone: snap a question, join a class, and read a chapter on the way home.

Records

School documents and results

Optional links to report cards and syllabus calendars so exam season is planned from real dates, not a blank page.

Future AI

AI roadmap

From a helpful chatbot to a personal study plan.

Magic Explain, quizzes, snap solve, and ब्रह्मांड Ai are the start. The next AI work makes the help follow the student, the chapter, and the exam date.

Near term

Answers that stay inside the book

Chapter-grounded replies that quote the NCERT section the student is reading, instead of a general web answer.

Near term

Weak-area quizzes

The next Micro Quiz is built from questions the student missed, so practice tightens the actual gap.

Next

A study plan with a date

Give an exam date and the subjects in scope. TutorBuddy drafts a week-by-week plan and adjusts it when a quiz slips.

Next

AI avatar lessons

Short taught lessons with an avatar that slows down, repeats, or changes the example when the learner is stuck.

Next

Spoken tutoring

A voice conversation for doubts and pronunciation, in more Indian languages than the current set.

Next

Notebook handwriting

Snap & Solve grows from printed questions to handwritten classwork and diagram labels.
